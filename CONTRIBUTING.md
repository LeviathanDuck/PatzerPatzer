# Contributing

Public bug reports and feature requests are welcome through this repository's structured issue
forms. Search existing issues first and keep reports focused on observable Chess Patzer product
behavior.

Do not include credentials, authorization material, private identifiers, account or game data,
PGN/FEN or move records, raw logs or request/response payloads, screenshots or attachments, local
computer paths, private development material, or hosting and deployment details.

Security concerns do not belong in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Development happens in a separate private repository. Accepted public changes are published here
with the corresponding source required by the project's license.
